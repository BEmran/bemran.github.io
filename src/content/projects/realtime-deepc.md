This project investigates Data-enabled Predictive Control using measured input-output data rather than relying primarily on an accurate mathematical model.

The initial study establishes standard DeePC experimentally, compares it against a baseline controller, and identifies failure modes associated with noise, changing operating conditions, uncertainty, constraints, and online computation. Later extensions may include adaptive or safe DeePC and hybrid data-driven/physics-based predictive models.
