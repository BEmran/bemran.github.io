This platform supports undergraduate experimentation in UAV control and autonomy using compact Crazyflie hardware, a 3D-printed constrained testbed, and software interfaces for MATLAB/Simulink and robotic experimentation.

The immediate objective is to establish a reliable project platform; research questions can then be layered on top once the hardware and software workflow is mature.
