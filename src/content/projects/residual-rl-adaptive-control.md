This completed foundation project studies a constrained residual reinforcement-learning layer that augments model-reference adaptive control rather than replacing it.

The work focuses on tracking under uncertainty, actuator safety, adaptation, and recovery after changing plant conditions. It serves as a foundation for the group's broader transition toward learning-augmented nonlinear control with explicit safety and uncertainty treatment.
