The custom 3-DOF platform constrains translation while allowing continuous roll, pitch, and yaw motion for safe laboratory experimentation.

It combines a modular mechanical structure, Raspberry Pi-based embedded control, onboard sensing, and a MATLAB/Simulink deployment workflow. The testbed supports education as well as repeated validation of classical, adaptive, robust, and learning-based control methods.
