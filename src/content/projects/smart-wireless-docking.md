This senior-design project treats robot charging as an integrated autonomous system rather than only a physical charger.

The prototype combines docking/alignment, wireless power transfer, charging-state detection, dock-robot communication, operational monitoring, and fault handling. The design is intended to support future extensions toward larger autonomous systems and mobility applications.
