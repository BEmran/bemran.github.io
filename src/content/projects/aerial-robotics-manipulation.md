This project develops a coupled nonlinear model and control framework for a tiltrotor UAV carrying a continuum manipulator.

The research addresses strong dynamic coupling, arm motion, changing configuration, disturbances, payload variation, actuator limits, and safe tracking. A staged experimental approach begins with a constrained bench testbed and progresses toward integrated aerial manipulation.
