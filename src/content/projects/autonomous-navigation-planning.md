This project develops navigation and planning algorithms that adapt to environmental risk while remaining computationally efficient and suitable for real robotic platforms.

Current work includes risk-adaptive extensions of the Dynamic Window Approach and learning-guided sampling strategies for RRT*. The longer-term objective is to connect planning decisions with uncertainty-aware control and experimental ROS 2 mobile-robot platforms.
