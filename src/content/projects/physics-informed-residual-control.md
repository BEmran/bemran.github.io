The project starts from a validated nominal nonlinear model and controller on the Quanser AERO platform. Controlled model mismatch and disturbances are then introduced to expose the residual dynamics between the model and the physical system.

A lightweight physics-informed residual learner estimates this discrepancy and is integrated with the nonlinear control framework. The evaluation emphasizes tracking, robustness, stability analysis, and real-time experimental implementation.
