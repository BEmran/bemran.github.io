shortBio: I am an Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering at the American University of Sharjah. My research focuses on nonlinear and adaptive control, learning-augmented control, autonomous robotics, and real-time experimental systems. I am particularly interested in methods that use learning to complement physics-based controllers while preserving stability, safety, robustness, and computational feasibility.
mentoringStatement: My research group follows a project-centered approach that connects control theory and learning methods to physical robotic platforms. Graduate and undergraduate researchers work across UAVs, mobile robots, experimental testbeds, and embedded real-time systems.
researchStatement: My current research program centers on uncertainty-aware safe learning-augmented control of nonlinear robotic systems. Related directions include physics-informed residual dynamics learning, data-enabled predictive control, control-barrier-function safety filters, adaptive and robust nonlinear control, and learning-guided navigation and motion planning.
