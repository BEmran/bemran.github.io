This research investigates predictive control methods that use measured input-output data directly rather than depending exclusively on a precise analytical model.

Initial work focuses on Data-enabled Predictive Control (DeePC) for laboratory robotic systems, including tracking, constraints, noise sensitivity, uncertainty, and computational feasibility. Longer-term directions include adaptive and safe DeePC, nonlinear data-driven models, Koopman representations, and integration with CBF-based safety mechanisms.
