This is the central research direction of the group: use learning where the physics model is incomplete, while retaining a theoretically grounded controller as the core of the closed loop.

The current emphasis is on learning residual or unmodeled dynamics, quantifying when the learned model is unreliable, and using that information inside safety-aware control mechanisms such as Control Barrier Functions. The goal is not to replace control theory with a black-box learner, but to obtain better tracking and adaptation without sacrificing stability, constraint satisfaction, interpretability, or real-time feasibility.

Representative topics include residual learning, conformal uncertainty calibration, safe online adaptation, actuator-aware control, and learning-augmented nonlinear control on physical robotic platforms.
