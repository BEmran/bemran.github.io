This area connects planning, navigation, perception, and control for autonomous robotic systems. Current work includes risk-adaptive local planning, learning-guided sampling-based planning, UAV autonomy, and experimental mobile-robot navigation.

The emphasis is on algorithms that remain computationally practical and interpretable, and that can be transferred from simulation to ROS 2/PX4-enabled experimental platforms.
