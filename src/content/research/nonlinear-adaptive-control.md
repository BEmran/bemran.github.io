This area develops controllers for systems whose dynamics are nonlinear, uncertain, coupled, or time-varying. The work builds on adaptive and robust control while moving toward modern safety-aware and learning-assisted formulations.

Current applications include UAVs, aerial manipulation, constrained experimental testbeds, and robotic systems with configuration-dependent dynamics. Particular attention is given to stability, disturbance rejection, actuator limits, and experimental validation.
